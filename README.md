## Research Projects
- Currently researching and exploring trustworthy and responsible AI and Human Computer Interaction.
- Medical Speech-to-Text for Clinical Dictation (In Progress): Developing speech recognition pipelines for clinical documentation, focusing on medical terminology, real-time transcription, and patient-data privacy.
- [Music Source Separation for Newari Traditional Instruments](https://github.com/JuJu2181/Newari_Music_Source_Separation): Exploring U-Net and GAN-based approaches for separating traditional Newari musical instruments using a self-curated dataset. Received the Best Abstract Award (Computer Science Track) at KHWOPA CEEL 2026.
- [Nepali Speech Recognition & Summarization](https://github.com/JuJu2181/Automatic-Nepali-Speech-Recognition-and-Summarizer): Developed and evaluated deep learning-based speech recognition and NLP pipelines for automatic Nepali transcription and summarization.
- [Automatic Nepali Number Plate Recognition System](https://github.com/JuJu2181/annpr): Developed a system that detects Nepali number plates from vehicles and then recognizes the number in the number plates.
- Nepali–English Code-Switched Speech Translation: Supervising research exploring multilingual and code-switched speech translation using modern speech models.
- Nepali OCR & Document Processing: Exploring AI-based approaches for optical character recognition in human written texts and document understanding.
